About the owner

Dr Satyam

I started Property_wallah more than ten years ago, after watching my uncle help families in Naugachia find land the honest way. He used to say a plot is not just a piece of earth, it is someone's whole saving.

Since then I have sat with many families at their kitchen tables, checked papers line by line and walked the boundaries myself. Some of those deals came back to me through the next generation.

Our line is simple: Satyaa hai, Bharosa hai. If a plot has a problem, I will tell you before you ask.

  • Local to Naugachia, not a distant agent
  • Papers explained in simple Hindi
  • Site visit before any payment talk
  • Brokerage told upfront, no surprises
  • Phone answered by the owner himself
